## VAT and global sales

The [FTA VAT registration guidance](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/vat.topics/registration.for.vat.aspx) sets mandatory registration at AED 375,000 of taxable supplies/imports in the previous 12 months or anticipated next 30 days; voluntary registration can apply above AED 187,500. Evaluate MAFSAN's whole taxable business, not only ApplyPilot. Existing registration/TRN and combined turnover are unknown.

Do not charge tax without confirming registration and tax treatment. Do not assume exported online services are universally zero-rated, or that foreign B2C digital sales have no local obligations. A global audience can create destination tax/consumer duties even when UAE registration thresholds are not met. Review customer-location evidence, UAE versus export supply treatment, invoices and applicable target jurisdictions before enabling sales there.

Exactly USD $9 remains the customer total. Any applicable tax must be included inside it; this reduces net product revenue. The implementation blocks countries until reviewed and supports manual inclusive Stripe tax-rate IDs without enabling a new paid tax subscription. It does not itself perform a global tax determination. Mismatched billed country must be caught/remedied; validate this in test Checkout before launch.

## Applicant privacy and DeepSeek

[Federal Decree Law 45/2021](https://www.uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download) is the federal personal-data framework. Identify whether the federal regime or a special free-zone regime applies, and assess relevant international customer laws before calling the product globally privacy-compliant.

The [DeepSeek privacy policy](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html) describes services and processing in China but expressly says downstream open-platform app processing rules are not covered by that policy. It therefore cannot establish this app's API retention/training or processor terms. Obtain the API-specific contractual arrangements, subprocessors, retention/deletion and transfer safeguards. Explicit consent, redaction and encryption do not automatically establish full compliance.

Free local review is available without uploading text. AI stays disabled until the applicable provider/transfer review is complete. Publish accurate controller details, purpose/lawful basis, processors, retention, transfer terms, rights/contact, breach process and backup deletion schedule before processing real CVs. Review sensitive identifiers and children restrictions. Keep only minimal accounting records separately from short-lived CV content.
